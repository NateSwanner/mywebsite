About

The titles were editorial.
The work became operational.

My career started in digital media, but the work increasingly became project and program management: launching products, redesigning workflows, coordinating engineering and commercial teams, managing budgets and capacity, resolving risk, and building operating systems.

I’ve worked across engineering, SEO, sales, commerce, design, executives, agencies, partners, full-time teams, and large freelance organizations. My strength is creating structure around complicated work—then keeping everyone aligned long enough to deliver.

How I Work

Structure first.
Then execution.

Complex work becomes manageable when the operating model is clear. Scroll through the five stages.

01

Discover

Clarify the business problem, gather requirements, define stakeholders, identify constraints, and establish measurable outcomes.

02

Plan

Define scope, workstreams, milestones, resources, capacity, risks, dependencies, delivery model, and reporting cadence.

03

Align

Create shared understanding, clarify ownership, establish governance and decision paths, and surface tradeoffs early.

04

Deliver

Track progress, manage risk and dependencies, control change, resolve blockers, communicate status, and adapt the plan.

05

Measure

Track outcomes, capture lessons learned, document repeatable processes, and improve the operating model.
